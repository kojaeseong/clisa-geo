Using the Site

Clisa Geopolitics analyzes major international developments, offers an assessment and outlook on each issue, attaches a probability to every forecast, and checks each one against the actual outcome on a set date. The diagram below shows that process; select any box to go to the corresponding page.

EventsBriefingsEach day, the key events in world affairs are selected and explained, alongside the related issues, assessments, and forecasts.→AssessmentIssue analysisStarts by establishing the facts, then assesses the situation and weighs the gains and losses of each option.→ScenariosScenariosMaps out the paths each issue could take through 2030 and assigns each a probability.→ForecastsProbabilistic forecastsPairs each assessment with a question whose outcome can be checked, a probability, and a resolution date.→VerificationForecasts and Track RecordWhen a forecast reaches its resolution date, checks it against what actually happened and shows whether it came true.

What each section covers

Global Overview
Each day's key developments appear in the briefing at the top. Select a country on the globe to see its current situation and foreign relations. The page also offers upcoming events, a summary of current developments, and a list of conflict zones.
Industry Outlook
Shows, scenario by scenario, how far five conflicts could push profits in 19 South Korean and U.S. industries above or below the current trend through 2030. The larger the dot, the more likely the scenario.
People's Lives
Uses 100 dots to show how likely five issues are to make people's lives better or worse through 2030. The developments that moved the charts are in the briefing on the Global Overview tab.
Features
In-depth pieces, each devoted to a single subject. The latest appears in full; earlier pieces are listed by title.
Strategic Analysis
For each issue: the facts, the positions of the countries involved, an assessment of the situation, an evaluation of the options, what it means for people, and how events may unfold through 2030, with forecasts.
World Order
Global scenarios through 2030, structural trends, and relations among the major powers.
Forecasts and Track Record
Every probabilistic forecast, grouped by issue, with its reasoning and resolution date.
Power Structures
Sets out, country by country, who decides and who can block a decision. The section brings together each decision-maker’s worldview and way of deciding, power under the constitution and power in practice, and the makeup of the legislature and what its members have said on each issue.
History and Ideas
Historical precedents structurally similar to the present, recurring patterns, and thinkers worth consulting.
Methodology
The procedures and rules by which assessments and forecasts are reached.
