Clisa Geopolitics analyzes world affairs from South Korea’s vantage point. For each major issue, from Northeast Asian security and great-power competition to conflicts in Europe and the Middle East, it starts from verified facts and works through an assessment of the situation, an evaluation of each strategic option, the consequences for individuals and businesses, and forecasts expressed as probabilities. The globe shows each country’s current situation alongside broader shifts in the world order.
