Israel's Knesset and elections

25th Knesset · Dissolved July 17, 2026 · Election October 27 · As of October 6, 2026

Results of the November 1, 2022, election. Splits and mergers have since changed the makeup of the chamber; at dissolution, the coalition was one or two seats short of a majority of 61 (Wikipedia [secondary source]).

Six polls released around September 27, 2026, gave the parties backing Prime Minister Netanyahu anywhere from 49 to 63 seats. Most put the bloc short of a majority (61 seats), but a Channel 14 poll projected 63. By party, Likud polled at 18–32 seats, Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar at 22–23, and Together, led by Naftali Bennett and Yair Lapid, at 7–13. The Arab parties' 12–14 seats could decide which side reaches 61.

Key players in the election

With the Knesset dissolved, what could change Israel's decision-maker is the October 27 election. The question is whether either the Netanyahu bloc or the anti-Netanyahu bloc can reach 61 seats.

The Haredi parties, which withdrew their support for the coalition over the draft in July 2025, secured the Basic Law provision on Torah study and the law barring arrests just before the Knesset dissolved. The Haredi parties' demand for draft exemptions and the far right's opposition to a ceasefire are likely to remain the price of their joining a Netanyahu coalition after the election. The polls broadly agree that the anti-Netanyahu bloc will struggle to reach 61 seats without the support of Arab parties.
