Bahrain says Iranian attack started fire at Sitra industrial site; IRGC claims it destroyed two U.S. radars

Bahrain's Interior Ministry said an Iranian attack on November 21 started a fire at a civilian industrial facility on Sitra Island, and the government said it had thwarted several other attacks (Al Jazeera). Iran's Revolutionary Guards claimed a missile and drone strike on U.S. bases at al-Riffa and Awali that destroyed two Patriot radars, in retaliation for an attack on a radar on Hengam Island; the claim has not been verified, and Iranian state media claimed the Sitra fire was caused by a falling U.S. interceptor missile (Al Jazeera).

IRGC claims two supertankers sailing unauthorized routes hit mines in Strait of Hormuz

Iran's Revolutionary Guards navy claimed on November 22 that two supertankers sailing unauthorized routes struck naval mines and exploded east of the Strait of Hormuz, north of Oman's Ghanam Island, and warned shipping companies to follow its navigation instructions (Xinhua, Press TV). The vessels' names and the damage have not been confirmed outside Iran.

Russian cargo ship hit in drone attack in Gulf of Finland; Finnish military says Ukraine likely responsible

Finland's Defense Ministry said a Russian-flagged cargo ship, the Sparta II, was hit by a drone attack on the morning of November 21 in international waters west of Gogland Island in the Gulf of Finland (Russian officials said it was bound for Kaliningrad), and the Finnish military said Ukraine was likely responsible (Reuters, AFP). The 16 crew members were evacuated; a Russian helicopter later intruded more than 4 kilometers into Finnish airspace, and Defense Minister Antti Häkkänen said the suspected violation may have been linked to rescue or tracking operations (Al Jazeera).

Iran says it held talks with Qatari mediators; Araghchi says the blockade must be lifted

Deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi said on November 21 that a Qatari delegation held two hours of talks with Iranian officials in Tehran on the night of November 20 but said they were not yet formal negotiations, and Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said the blockade must be lifted “rationally” and that discussing the reopening of the Strait of Hormuz was pointless as long as Iran's demands went unmet (AFP, Reuters). President Donald Trump told Fox News the same day that he wants a “permanent” deal that keeps Iran from getting a nuclear weapon (AFP, Reuters).

Zelensky says Russia fired about 2,700 drones and 165 missiles in a week, urges U.S. to approve Patriot deliveries

President Volodymyr Zelensky said on November 22 that Russia had launched about 2,700 attack drones, about 1,460 guided bombs, and 165 missiles over the past week, and urged Washington to approve delivery of Patriot interceptors that allies had already paid for (Al Jazeera). Early that day, a Russian strike partly collapsed an apartment building in Dymer, north of Kyiv, and rescuers recovered two bodies (Al Jazeera).

Russian vessel at Libya's Benghazi naval base explodes in suspected sea drone attack

Libya's eastern government said a Russian vessel docked at the Benghazi naval base to refuel and take on supplies was hit on November 22 by a suspected sea drone, causing an explosion and fire, and that it had opened a joint investigation with Russia (Reuters, Al Jazeera). An unknown number of people were injured, and no one has claimed responsibility.

Mexico captures FBI Ten Most Wanted fugitive ‘El Chapo Isidro’

Mexico's Defense Ministry and the U.S. Justice Department announced on November 21 that Mexican troops had arrested Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as “El Chapo Isidro,” a senior Sinaloa Cartel leader on the FBI's Ten Most Wanted list, in Guasave, Sinaloa, on November 20 (U.S. Justice Department, Al Jazeera). Mexico's Defense Ministry said the operation was coordinated with U.S. intelligence agencies, and the Justice Department said U.S. agencies supported it.

Asian stocks slide amid AI bubble fears; Kospi falls as much as 5.8%

Amid mounting fears of an AI bubble, South Korea's Kospi fell as much as 5.8 percent on November 23 and was down 3.2 percent as of 4:30 a.m. GMT, while Hong Kong's Hang Seng dropped about 0.6 percent (Al Jazeera). On November 20, the S&P 500 had fallen 3.7 percent and the Nasdaq Composite 5.3 percent (Al Jazeera).

Netanyahu and Eisenkot meet on coalition but reach no deal

Caretaker Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Yashar leader Gadi Eisenkot, who holds the mandate to form a government, met in Herzliya on November 22 but reached no deal and agreed only to meet again (Channel 12, AP). Eisenkot has until December 6 to form a government, with a possible 14-day extension (Al Jazeera).
