October 7, 2026

Russia hits Kyiv with missiles before dawn on October 7, killing two

Explosions were heard in Kyiv at about 3:15 a.m. and again at about 5:15 a.m. local time on October 7, after Ukraine's Air Force warned that several Russian Tu-95 bombers were approaching the border. Kyiv Mayor Vitali Klitschko reported strikes on nonresidential areas in the Obolon and Shevchenkivskyi districts and damage to a warehouse in the Podil district, and the Kyiv City Military Administration said two people were killed in a fire at a garage cooperative in the Darnytskyi district. As of publication, Ukraine's Air Force had not released a full tally, and Russia's Defense Ministry had not issued a statement.

Bearing on the assessmentsThe strike came hours after President Volodymyr Zelensky warned on the night of October 6 that Russia was preparing a massive attack, and it follows Ukraine's drone barrage on the Moscow area, as the two sides trade strikes on each other's capitals. That fits the continuing war of attrition described in Conclusion 1 of the Russia-Ukraine War analysis and weighs against the forecast that a full Russia-Ukraine ceasefire will take effect.

North Korea's Foreign Ministry calls Seoul's mine findings a farce, rules out talks with the South on military sovereignty

In a statement carried by the Korean Central News Agency on October 6, North Korea's Foreign Ministry claimed that the October 5 final findings of South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff had exposed the purpose of what it called a farce planned and staged by Seoul. It warned that if South Korean troops, or even a spark from them, crossed even one millimeter into North Korean territory, its border forces would respond immediately and forcefully. The ministry said Pyongyang would under no circumstances discuss the exercise of military sovereignty on its own territory with a hostile state and would defend that territory through force rather than negotiation, reaffirming this as the government's official position.

Bearing on the assessmentsBy restating as official policy that it will not treat South Korea as a negotiating partner on military matters, Pyongyang has moved closer to the formalization condition in the first disconfirming signal of the Korean Peninsula analysis. (The missile-launch-frequency condition is tracked separately in the weekly review.) That is a factor raising the odds of the forecast that inter-Korean military hotlines will not be restored.

Drones hit two merchant ships in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone, sinking one

According to Bulgarian broadcaster Nova, the Togo-flagged Alfa Watan and the Palau-flagged Able were struck by drones on October 6 in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone. The Alfa Watan sank, and all 18 crew members of the Able were rescued. It was not clear who launched the drones. The same day, Odesa Oblast Governor Oleh Kiper said one person was killed aboard a Marshall Islands-flagged ship in a Russian attack.

Bearing on the assessmentsA day after an attack off Romania on October 5, merchant ships came under attack in the exclusive economic zone of Bulgaria, another NATO member, widening the danger to Black Sea shipping. That is consistent with the continuing war of attrition described in Conclusion 1 of the Russia-Ukraine War analysis and weighs against the forecast that a full Russia-Ukraine ceasefire will take effect.

Ukraine launches mass drone attack on Moscow area; two killed in surrounding region

Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said 650 drones headed toward Moscow and the surrounding region from 8:30 p.m. on October 5 to 5 a.m. on October 6, Moscow time, and that most were shot down. Moscow region authorities said two people were killed. TASS, tallying Russian Defense Ministry reports, counted 868 drones downed overnight. The same night, Ukraine's Air Force said Russia had launched 137 drones and that 114 were shot down or neutralized.

Bearing on the assessmentsUkraine is stepping up attacks on Russia's capital region, which fits the continuing war of attrition described in Conclusion 1 of the Russia-Ukraine War analysis. That weighs against the forecast that a full Russia-Ukraine ceasefire will take effect.

Foreign Minister Cho Hyun says Ukraine's apology falls somewhat short, weighs recalling ambassador

Testifying before the National Assembly's Foreign Affairs and Unification Committee during its annual audit on October 6, Foreign Minister Cho Hyun said he had received a message containing an apology from Ukraine's foreign minister over Zelensky's disclosure, in his September 23 speech to the U.N. General Assembly, that two North Korean prisoners of war had been sent to South Korea. "It falls somewhat short of the level we had in mind, so we intend to take firm measures," Cho said, adding that recalling South Korea's ambassador to Ukraine is among the options "under review."

Bearing on the assessmentsConclusion 6 of the Korean Peninsula analysis, which takes individual lives as its yardstick, puts humanitarian issues such as the return of detainees first. Here, a humanitarian matter, the transfer of North Korean prisoners of war to the South, has turned into a diplomatic dispute with a third country. It does not move the probability of any specific forecast.

Saudi-led coalition says it intercepted a ballistic missile aimed at Khamis Mushait

Turki al-Maliki, spokesman for the Saudi-led coalition, said on X on October 6 that coalition forces had intercepted and destroyed a ballistic missile aimed at Khamis Mushait, a city in southern Saudi Arabia that hosts an air base. The announcement came a day after the Houthis claimed attacks on airports and oil refining facilities on the Saudi mainland. Brent crude dipped below $100 a barrel in trading the same day.

Bearing on the assessmentsThere is still no confirmed large-scale destruction of Gulf oil facilities, the disconfirming signal for Conclusion 2 of the Iran and Hormuz analysis. Continued attacks aimed at the Saudi mainland have raised the risk of escalation, yet oil prices fell, so the factors bearing on the forecast that Brent will end the year below $90 a barrel point in both directions.
