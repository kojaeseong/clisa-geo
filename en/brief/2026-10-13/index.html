North Korea fires ballistic missiles; state media says it tested a tactical ballistic missile

South Korea's Joint Chiefs of Staff said North Korea fired ballistic missiles from the Pyongyang area into the East Sea at about 9:20 a.m. on October 12, and that one of them flew more than 900 kilometers. The state-run Korean Central News Agency reported on October 13 that the Missile Administration had test-fired a tactical ballistic missile.

Trump says he "may" meet Kim Jong Un on Asia trip

Asked by a reporter at the White House on October 12 whether he plans to meet Kim Jong Un during next month's trip to the APEC summit in Shenzhen, China, President Trump said, "I may." No meeting has been announced.

Zelensky accuses Russia of violating energy ceasefire; Kremlin says contacts continue

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, addressing the Warsaw Security Forum by video link on October 12, said Russia had violated the energy ceasefire announced by President Trump. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said the same day only that contacts were continuing on whether Russia would agree to the truce.

Kremlin rejects Zelensky's 60-day election ceasefire offer

After Zelensky said on October 11 that Ukraine would hold elections if given a 60-day total ceasefire, Peskov said on October 12 that President Vladimir Putin was ready to halt strikes on election day only. He claimed Ukraine would use a truce to regroup and keep fighting.

IRGC claims it struck a third supertanker in the Strait of Hormuz

Iran's Revolutionary Guard navy claimed on October 12 that it struck a supertanker that ignored warnings and tried to leave the Strait of Hormuz, the third tanker it said it had hit for violating its rules. It did not name the vessel or its flag, and there has been no independent confirmation.

Houthis fire missiles at Riyadh and Khamis Mushait; coalition says it intercepted them

The Saudi-led coalition said it intercepted a Houthi ballistic missile fired at Riyadh on October 12 and, just after midnight on October 13, two more aimed at Khamis Mushait in southern Saudi Arabia. Houthi politburo member Mohammed al-Bukhaiti warned that if the U.S. joins Saudi airstrikes on Yemen, the group will retaliate against the U.S. and its interests in the region.

29 nations, including the U.S. and UK, condemn Iran's violent attacks and hybrid activity

In a joint statement on October 12, foreign ministers from the U.S., the UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Norway, and 23 EU member states condemned "in the strongest terms" Iran's violent attacks and hybrid activity. The statement recognized Britain's attribution of Iran's involvement in the September 27 incident at RAF Fairford.

Brent rises 1.8 percent to settle at $106.65 a barrel

Brent crude for December delivery settled at $106.65 a barrel on October 12, up 1.8 percent. Market reports cited the continued shutdown of Riyadh's airport, reports of tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz, and doubts about the Russia-Ukraine energy ceasefire.

EU sanctions 826 Russian defense firms and 743 CEOs in biggest listing since 2022

The Council of the EU on October 12 added to its sanctions list 826 companies in Russia's military-industrial complex that supply components for missiles, drones, aircraft, and more, along with their 743 chief executives. High Representative Kaja Kallas called it "the largest round of sanctions listings since 2022."
