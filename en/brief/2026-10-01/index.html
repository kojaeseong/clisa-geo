Defense minister says he believes North Korean troops crossed the MDL to lay mines

Defense Minister Kang Shin-chul told the National Assembly's National Defense Committee on September 29, 2026, that the DMZ explosion had indeed been caused by a North Korean mine and was indeed a violation of the Armistice Agreement. The same day, the military said it had found another mine about 1 meter from the blast site, indicating that at least four mines had been buried 1 to 2 meters apart, and Kang said it appeared that North Korean troops had crossed the Military Demarcation Line to bury them. Kang believes the mines were laid around the time of North Korea's border fortification work in 2024.

Bearing on the assessmentsThe incident cuts against the conditions that would change Conclusion 1 of the Korean Peninsula analysis (restored military hotlines and reunions of separated families) and raises f15, the forecast that the hotlines will not be restored. The next thing to watch is whether loudspeaker broadcasts toward the North resume, which the government has said it will not rule out.

Cabinet approves special bill on nuclear-powered submarines

A Cabinet meeting chaired by President Lee Jae-myung on September 29, 2026, approved a special bill governing the acquisition, operation and safety management of nuclear-powered submarines. The government said it would set military nuclear safety standards separate from the existing Defense Acquisition Program Act and Nuclear Safety Act in order to keep the confidence of the United States and the International Atomic Energy Agency, and it will submit the bill to the National Assembly with the aim of enacting it this year. The same day, North Korea's Foreign Ministry accused South Korea of pursuing nuclear proliferation.

Bearing on the assessmentsThe step bears on the conditional nuclear latency (A3b) in Conclusion 3 of the Korean Peninsula analysis. Forecast f17, that China will not impose THAAD-style large-scale economic retaliation over South Korea's pursuit of enrichment, reprocessing and nuclear-powered submarines, will not be moved in either direction until Beijing responds officially.

Iran says it has received U.S. reply to its proposal to end the war

At a Cabinet meeting on September 30, 2026, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi briefed President Masoud Pezeshkian on the U.S. reply to Iran's proposal to end the war. Pezeshkian said, "We will make every effort to bring the agreement to fruition," and added that any agreement must benefit both sides, but the content of the reply was not disclosed. Trump had rejected as unacceptable Iran's September 23 proposal to reopen the strait within a week if the United States lifted its port blockade, returned frozen assets and waived oil sanctions.

Bearing on the assessmentsThe phased exchange described in Conclusion 7 of the Iran and Hormuz analysis, reopening the strait in return for lifting the blockade and sanctions, is now being traded on paper. Because it is unclear whether the U.S. reply also demands action on the nuclear issue, no direction is set for f38, the phased-agreement forecast, or f03, the ceasefire-and-reopening forecast.

U.S. completes troop withdrawal from Iraq

The Pentagon announced on September 30, 2026, that the withdrawal of U.S. troops from Iraq, which had numbered about 2,500, was complete. Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell called it the success of a 12-year campaign that defeated the Islamic State (ISIS) as an organized military threat, and Trump called the U.S. presence in Iraq a "quagmire." The withdrawal comes as the Iran war enters its seventh month.

Bearing on the assessmentsAmid the prolonged blockade standoff described in Conclusion 1 of the Iran and Hormuz analysis, the United States has pulled its ground forces out of a country bordering Iran. It does not directly move the forecast that the U.S. will resume airstrikes on Iran's mainland in either direction.

Russia hits power grid around Kyiv ahead of winter

Russian missile and drone attacks overnight on September 30, 2026, killed three people in the city of Kyiv and a child in the surrounding Kyiv region. Zelensky said the attacks targeted energy facilities and damaged the power grid; energy facilities in the Zhytomyr, Mykolaiv, Kirovohrad and Rivne regions and the port of Izmail were also hit. Russia's Defense Ministry confirmed the strikes.

Bearing on the assessmentsThe attacks uphold the assessment in Conclusion 1 of the Russia-Ukraine War analysis that the war of attrition will continue. Russia has not been confirmed to accept the U.S.-backed halt to mutual strikes on energy facilities, and no date has been announced for the trilateral technical-level talks in forecast f02, which resolves on October 31.
