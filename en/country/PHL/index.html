Southeast Asia

Philippines

President Ferdinand Marcos Jr.

As ASEAN chair in 2026, the Philippines is pushing to conclude a code of conduct for the South China Sea, yet its clashes with China at Scarborough Shoal and Second Thomas Shoal are intensifying instead. On September 25, Chinese navy and coast guard vessels openly blocked a Philippine resupply mission bound for Second Thomas Shoal, the first such blockade since a provisional arrangement in 2024. At home, the feud between the Marcos and Duterte clans has peaked with impeachment proceedings against Vice President Sara Duterte and a warrant for her arrest.

Grand strategy

Maritime sovereignty and domestic political stability.

Must achieve

  • Giving effect to its South China Sea rights (the 2016 arbitral ruling)
  • Strengthening the U.S. alliance
  • Concluding an ASEAN code of conduct (as 2026 chair)

Can concede

  • Adjusting how resupply missions are carried out
  • Economic cooperation with China

Will never concede

  • Withdrawing the BRP Sierra Madre from Second Thomas Shoal
  • Abandoning the arbitral ruling

The Marcos government is pushing back with the U.S. alliance and a transparency strategy that publicizes footage of Chinese conduct. China's blockade of the September 25 resupply mission broke the 2024 provisional arrangement, raising the risk of clashes again.

Recent developments

  • September 25, 2026Chinese warships (including two Type 022 missile boats) and coast guard vessels blocked a Philippine resupply mission bound for Ayungin (Second Thomas) Shoal with dangerous maneuvers, forcing it to abort, the first open blockade since the provisional arrangement of July 2024.
  • September 18, 2026A Chinese coast guard vessel rammed a ship of the Philippine Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources 54 nautical miles west of Palawan, inside the Philippine exclusive economic zone (EEZ).
  • September 4, 2026A court issued an arrest warrant for Vice President Sara Duterte on charges of threatening to kill the president (a Senate impeachment trial is also under way).
  • July 23, 2026The day after Foreign Minister Wang Yi warned at an ASEAN meeting in Manila of bitter consequences, vessels from the two countries faced off again at Scarborough Shoal.

What to watch

  • A renewed resupply attempt at Second Thomas Shoal and the threshold for invoking the U.S.-Philippine Mutual Defense Treaty
  • Progress in code of conduct (COC) negotiations under the Philippines' ASEAN chairmanship
  • How the vice president's impeachment and criminal case shape the 2028 presidential race
