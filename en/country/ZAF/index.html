Africa

South Africa

President Cyril Ramaphosa

The ANC-led Government of National Unity (GNU), a 10-party coalition, has held together for two and a half years, but Ramaphosa is fighting impeachment proceedings over the Phala Phala scandal in the courts. After excluding South Africa from the G20, the United States in September imposed visa sanctions on officials over the country's land and race policies. The November 4 local elections will be the GNU's first test. Meanwhile, unemployment has soared to 33.6 percent.

Recent developments

  • September 22, 2026Ramaphosa skipped the UN General Assembly, citing health reasons.
  • September 16, 2026U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio announced visa restrictions on South African officials, citing racial discrimination and land expropriation without compensation.
  • August 5, 2026Parliament's impeachment committee decided to appoint Thuli Madonsela as evidence leader.
  • July 24, 2026Ramaphosa obtained an interim order halting the impeachment inquiry until judicial review of the Phala Phala report is complete.

What to watch

  • ANC and DA vote shares in the November 4 local elections, and whether the GNU survives
  • The outcome of the judicial review of the Phala Phala report and the resumption of impeachment proceedings
  • Deteriorating relations with the United States (tariffs, visa sanctions, exclusion from the G20)
