Latin America

Mexico

President Claudia Sheinbaum (Morena)

Since the killing of CJNG boss "El Mencho" in February, the Sheinbaum government has eased U.S. pressure for military intervention through a hard-line security policy and extraditions to the United States. Washington has refused to extend the USMCA and is maintaining tariffs of 50 percent on steel and aluminum and 25 percent on non-originating autos; the two countries resume trade negotiations in Washington on September 28.

Recent developments

  • September 23, 2026Mexico stayed out of the U.S.-led anti-cartel coalition, the Shield of the Americas, but its major cartels are among the 24 groups on the coalition's list of narco-terrorist organizations
  • September 16, 2026After her 22nd call with Trump, Sheinbaum said a trade deal was making progress, and talks were set to resume in Washington on September 28
  • August 28, 2026U.S. forces shot down three cartel drones near the border with a laser weapon
  • July 1, 2026Washington's refusal to extend the USMCA for 16 years triggered a regime of annual reviews; Mexico said it supports an extension

What to watch

  • Whether steel and auto tariffs come down at the September 28 trade talks in Washington
  • The possibility of unilateral U.S. strikes on cartels on Mexican soil
  • The CJNG succession struggle after El Mencho's death and deteriorating security
