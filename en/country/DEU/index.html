Europe

Germany

Chancellor Friedrich Merz (CDU) / President Frank-Walter Steinmeier

With military spending of $114 billion in 2025, Germany ranked fourth in the world, and by stationing a permanent armored brigade in Lithuania it has become the center of European rearmament. In September's state elections, the AfD took 43.8 percent in Saxony-Anhalt; in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, the AfD came first and the CDU fell below 5 percent; and in Berlin, the Left Party finished first. These were the worst results ever for parties in government, and they have badly sapped the momentum of Merz's coalition. Berlin formally blamed Russia for an incident involving explosive-laden drones at Leipzig airport, stepping up its response to Russian hybrid warfare.

Recent developments

  • September 20, 2026The AfD came first in Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, where the CDU failed to clear the 5 percent threshold, and in Berlin the Left Party finished first for the first time. Merz acknowledged the results as a disaster but declared that the coalition would continue.
  • September 6, 2026In the Saxony-Anhalt state election, the AfD won a landslide 43.8 percent but fell short of a majority. The CDU managed only 17.2 percent.
  • September 1, 2026Germany for the first time formally blamed Russia for an incident involving explosive-laden drones near Leipzig/Halle airport. Russia retaliated by closing the Goethe-Institut.
  • June 17, 2026Germany signed a bilateral defense cooperation agreement with Poland.

What to watch

  • Whether the firewall against the AfD holds as governments are formed in Saxony-Anhalt and Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania
  • The Merz coalition's reform agenda and pressure on the SPD to walk away
  • How quickly the Lithuania brigade reaches full strength, and the spread of Russian hybrid attacks (drones, sabotage)
