Israel

Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu (President Isaac Herzog)

Israel is fighting on multiple fronts: waging the war on Iran alongside the United States, occupying a buffer zone in southern Lebanon south of the Litani River and continuing air strikes in Syria. In Gaza, the Board of Peace announced a deal in late July for Hamas's phased disarmament, but implementation is uncertain. The Knesset has dissolved, and a general election is set for October 27.

Grand strategy

The physical security of the state: ensuring that no hostile force acquires the ability to threaten its existence.

Must achieve

  • Permanent elimination of Iran's nuclear capability
  • Disarmament of Hezbollah and Hamas
  • Normalization of relations with Saudi Arabia

Can concede

  • How Gaza is governed (international administration, for example)
  • The timing of withdrawal from parts of the occupied territories
  • Tactical concessions in coordination with Washington

Will never concede

  • A nuclear-armed Iran
  • Handing control of its security to outsiders
  • The rebuilding of armed groups near its borders

Israel has extended its preemptive principle—eliminate threats before they grow—to Iranian soil. It maintains buffer zones in southern Lebanon and southern Syria and sustains several fronts at once. The October 27 election is the variable that will decide whether this strategy endures.
