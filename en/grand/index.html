World scenarios through 2030

  • Managed competition 35% · The U.S.-China tariff truce is extended, the Russia-Ukraine war freezes without a legal end, and an interim compromise with Iran trades nuclear limits for lifting the blockade.
  • Armed multipolarity 30% · The U.S. retreat accelerates and allies arm themselves.
  • Protracted linked wars 15% · U.S. airstrikes on Iran resume after November and spread across the Gulf.
  • Taiwan Strait crisis 10% · The crisis arrives not as a full-scale invasion but as Chinese coast guard inspections and quarantine or a partial blockade.
  • Internal fracture in one pole 10% · Russia's war economy collapses, the Iranian regime falls, or China suffers a financial crisis.
